Privacy Policy

Jegnaye (ጀግናዬ) · Effective January 2026 · App Store public policy page

Your privacy and discretion matter. This Privacy Policy explains how Jegnaye handles information.

1. Information we collect

  • Onboarding answers — fitness goals, pelvic control, and lifestyle habits used to personalize coaching. Stored on device.
  • Optional contact info — phone or email if you provide it for payment verification when that feature is enabled.
  • Payment receipt images — only if you upload a receipt when payments are enabled.
  • Push token — only if you allow notifications (optional; not required for core coaching).
  • Anonymous diagnostics — device model / OS version for stability.

2. On-device storage

Workout history, streaks, and lesson completions are stored in the app sandbox on your device.

3. No selling of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or health-related data to brokers or advertisers. We do not use health-related answers for advertising.

4. Deletion

Delete local account data anytime via Settings → Delete Account & All Data. Deletion from the device is immediate.

5. Children

Jegnaye is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

6. Contact

Email: support@jegnaye.com
